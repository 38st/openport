00000200Install

Try it in one command

docker run --rm -p 127.0.0.1:8080:8080 -v openport:/var/lib/openport ghcr.io/38st/openport

With Docker installed, this starts Cboe’s free 15-minute delayed feed for SPX, SPY, QQQ, IWM and DIA. No key needed. Open the link it prints, http://localhost:8080/#token=…. The volume keeps your accounts and chart history between runs. When nothing is trading, choose the demo market’s simulated prices.

On the default delayed feed, a pass is practice, not proof: a real-time chart shows where the market went next. Funded plans stay hidden in the terminal unless already funded or explicitly enabled; OpenPort funds no one.

00000400Analytics

Analytics you can audit

OpenPort computes its own implied volatility and Greeks, fits SVI surfaces, and shows gamma and vanna exposure. The forward and discount factor come from put-call parity; the provider’s IV stays alongside for comparison.

iv solve
about 0.4 µs · 5.4 Newton iterations per option
spx pass
about 40 ms · 30,182 options, 63 expiries
Δ vs Cboe, SPX
0.012 vol points
Δ vs Cboe, QQQ
0.030 vol points
Δ vs Cboe, SPY
0.028 vol points

Medians on an Apple M2 Max on 2026-09-24: the IV solve from openport_bench, the SPX pass over 23 passes on live data. Differences are against Cboe’s published IVs during the session, on out-of-the-money options within 10% of the forward. SVI fits run separately, on demand in the API.

Exposure assumes dealers are long calls and short puts: a modelling convention, not knowledge of anyone’s positions.

00000600Your machine

Your data stays yours

One C++20 binary runs the feed, analytics, simulator and web terminal. Self-host it with your own keys. Your data and trades stay on your machine unless you enable external notifications.

free
Cboe, 15-minute delayed
adapters
Databento · Massive · ThetaData · Tradier or tastytrade market data

The adapters follow their documented APIs and are tested against sample responses, but have not yet been run live with a key or account. The broker adapters read market data only; they never send orders.
